The Reloading Podcast

All about the cartridge reloading hobby — from beginner with nothing to the advanced handloader, we cover all the topics.

Standard disclaimer, folks: you'll probably hear, from episode to episode, recipes for what we do. Remember — we're telling you what we do; we're not telling you what you should do. What you should do is stick with published loads. We disavow any responsibility for damages done, because you might not be following a safe and careful reloading practice that Jeremy, I, Jason, Trevor, or Mike might be following. It could be that you have a tighter chamber, a looser rifling — you might not get the velocity we do, or you might get more. If you're going to reload, understand: you're responsible for what you do. We are not.
